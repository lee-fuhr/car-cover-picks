A good car cover is the cheapest insurance you can buy for your vehicle, shielding paint from UV rays, bird droppings, tree sap, and harsh winter weather. But not all covers are created equal, some breathe well but leak, others are waterproof but trap moisture, and many just don’t fit securely. When choosing, focus on three things: material layering (more layers generally mean better protection but less breathability), fit (snug with mirror pockets or straps), and your primary climate (snow and rain call for a heavier outer shell, while sun requires UV resistance).
